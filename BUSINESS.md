# 💼 Business Analysis — Sprint Day 2 Todo App

## 🎯 The Wedge

**Problem:** Existing todo apps (Todoist, Notion, TickTick) are either:
- Too complex for casual users (Notion)
- Too expensive for students/freelancers (Todoist Premium $4/mo)
- Lack modern, beautiful design
- Have poor mobile experience

**Our Wedge:** A **beautiful, fast, free** todo app that focuses on the essentials:
- Clean, modern UI (Todoist-inspired but better)
- Instant sync across devices
- Priority-based workflow
- Zero learning curve
- **Free forever** for core features

**Target Audience:**
- Students (18-25) who can't afford Todoist Premium
- Freelancers who need simple task management
- Remote workers tired of bloated project management tools
- Anyone who wants a beautiful, fast todo app

## 🚀 Activation Moment

**The "Aha!" moment happens when:**

1. **User signs up** (< 30 seconds)
2. **Creates first todo** with priority (< 10 seconds)
3. **Sees instant sync** across devices
4. **Completes first task** and sees the satisfying animation

**Time to activation:** < 60 seconds from landing page to first completed task

**Key metrics:**
- Signup → First todo: < 2 minutes
- First todo → Second todo: < 5 minutes
- Day 1 retention: 40%+ (industry standard: 25%)

## 💰 Pricing Strategy

### Free Tier (Forever)
- Unlimited todos
- 3 priority levels
- Basic filters and sorting
- Mobile + desktop sync
- **Target:** 80% of users

**Why free?**
- Build user base quickly
- Word-of-mouth growth
- Data for product improvements
- Upsell to premium later

### Premium Tier ($3/month or $30/year)
- Custom priority levels
- Due date reminders
- Recurring tasks
- Team collaboration (up to 5 members)
- Advanced analytics
- Custom themes
- Priority support
- **Target:** 5-10% conversion rate

### Team Tier ($8/user/month)
- Everything in Premium
- Unlimited team members
- Admin controls
- SSO integration
- API access
- Dedicated support
- **Target:** 1-2% of users

## 📊 Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Organic (70% of users):**
- SEO + content marketing: $0.50/user
- Word-of-mouth: $0/user
- **Blended CAC (organic):** $0.35/user

**Paid (30% of users):**
- Google Ads: $2.50/user
- Social media ads: $1.80/user
- **Blended CAC (paid):** $2.15/user

**Overall CAC:** $0.89/user

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Free users:**
- Direct revenue: $0
- Indirect value: Word-of-mouth, data, potential upsell
- **LTV:** $0 (but valuable for growth)

**Premium users:**
- Monthly: $3/mo × 18 months avg = $54
- Annual: $30/year × 2.5 years avg = $75
- **Blended LTV:** $64.50

**Team users:**
- $8/user/mo × 5 users × 24 months = $960
- **LTV:** $960/team

### LTV:CAC Ratio

**Premium users:**
- LTV: $64.50
- CAC: $0.89
- **Ratio: 72:1** (excellent, target is 3:1)

**Team users:**
- LTV: $960
- CAC: $50 (higher touch sales)
- **Ratio: 19:1** (excellent)

## 📈 Growth Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch (Months 1-3)
- **Goal:** 1,000 users
- **Strategy:** Product Hunt launch, Reddit, Twitter
- **Budget:** $0 (organic only)
- **Metrics:** 40% D1 retention, 20% W1 retention

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 4-12)
- **Goal:** 50,000 users
- **Strategy:** SEO, content marketing, paid ads
- **Budget:** $5,000/month

### Phase 3: Scale (Year 2)
- **Goal:** 500,000 users
- **Strategy:** Partnerships, integrations, team features
- **Budget:** $50,000/month
- **Metrics:** 5% premium conversion, $150K MRR

## 🎯 Key Differentiators

### vs. Todoist
- **Cheaper:** $3/mo vs $4/mo
- **Better design:** Modern glassmorphism vs dated UI
- **Faster:** Optimistic UI, instant sync
- **Simpler:** Focus on essentials, not complexity

### vs. Notion
- **Focused:** Todo app, not a wiki
- **Faster:** No loading spinners
- **Easier:** Zero learning curve
- **Mobile-first:** Better mobile experience

### vs. TickTick
- **Prettier:** Modern design vs cluttered UI
- **Cheaper:** $3/mo vs $3.99/mo
- **Simpler:** Less features = easier to use
